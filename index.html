May 17 morning logistics — car & bags
Before leaving for Magic Kingdom
Step 1: Drop bags at POFQ front desk (Bell Services). Disney transfers them to Boardwalk Inn while we're at MK.

Step 2: Drive the Tesla from POFQ to Boardwalk Inn. Park the car there for the duration of the Disney stay — it's walking distance to EPCOT and a direct bus to MK, HS, AK, and EPCOT all run from Boardwalk.

Step 3: Take the Boardwalk Inn bus directly to Magic Kingdom. Boardwalk is an EPCOT-area resort — buses run directly to all four parks including MK main entrance. No transfer needed.

Step 4: End of MK night — Minnie Van or rideshare from inside MK to Boardwalk Inn. Check in. Bags will be waiting.
Confirmed rules learned during booking
✓ HS Multi Pass with AK first park: Confirmed by Disney cast member. You CAN pre-select HS rides at 7 AM while physically entering AK first.

✓ One Multi Pass per day total: Cannot buy both AK and HS Multi Passes. Chose HS — correct call given Thrill-Data sell-out times.

✓ Single Pass does not trigger rolling chain: Only Multi Pass redemptions start the rolling chain. FoP at AK triggers nothing. Rolling chain begins at HS after Slinky tap at 3:10 PM.

✓ Grace period: officially ±15 minutes per Disney cast member. Arrive within your window.

The mass exodus — what we're dealing with
Magic Kingdom end-of-night crush
MK hosts up to 80,000 guests. After Happily Ever After (~17 min, ~9 PM), most attempt to exit simultaneously. Main Street: shoulder-to-shoulder. Monorail + bus lines: 45–60+ min. This is one of the worst exit experiences at any theme park on Earth. We plan around it intentionally. Being well-rested for AK early entry at 7:30 AM tomorrow is a completely valid reason to leave early.
The two-TRON strategy
TRON Ride 1 (3:15–4:15 PM Single Pass) + TRON Ride 2 (fireworks standby ~9 PM)
TRON standby drops to its daily floor (~20–35 min) during the 17-minute fireworks show while the entire park stands still on Main Street. We are in Tomorrowland moving the opposite direction. At 8:45 PM, walk toward TRON standby queue. Ride exits around 9:25–9:35 PM. At 9:20 PM (while still on the ride or exiting): open Lyft in MDE and request a Minnie Van to Boardwalk Inn.
Exit options — group decides on the night
Option A — The TRON Maneuver
Recommended if staying late
8:45 PM: Walk to TRON standby. Ride during fireworks (20–35 min wait). Exit ~9:25 PM. Request Minnie Van to Boardwalk Inn from inside the park. Minnie Van pickup is inside the park gates — bypasses the Main Street crush entirely. Cost: ~$35–50, split four ways = ~$9–13 each.
Option B — Leave before fireworks
Smart AK prep call
Exit around 8:30–8:45 PM before the exodus begins. Request Minnie Van or rideshare to Boardwalk Inn. AK early entry is 7:30 AM tomorrow — being well-rested is worth more than a second TRON ride. No shame in calling it early. This is arguably the smartest call given what tomorrow requires.
Option C — California Grill (if reservation kept)
Best fireworks scenario
Exit by 7:15 PM, walk 15–20 min to Contemporary, arrive for 8:20 PM reservation. Disney dims lights and pauses service for fireworks — watch Happily Ever After from the restaurant rooftop over the Seven Seas Lagoon. Crowd-free, cocktail in hand. Forfeit second TRON ride. Truly spectacular if the reservation stays.
Walk to Contemporary — backup rideshare option

If Minnie Van isn't available: exit front gates (~9:40 PM), walk 15–20 min north to the Contemporary Resort, call Lyft/Uber from the Contemporary's address. Far less chaotic than the MK rideshare staging zone. The Outer Rim bar is there if the group wants a drink while waiting.

TENTATIVE — May 20–21 · Universal Orlando

This section is a planning framework. Details, LL/Express strategy, and specific times will be refined as we get closer. All content subject to update.

Critical — Epic Universe cannot park hop with IOA or USF
Epic Universe is on a completely separate campus
Epic Universe is located ~15 minutes south of Universal's main property (Islands of Adventure + Universal Studios Florida + CityWalk). Park-hopping between Epic Universe and the other parks is not available. Each campus must be its own dedicated day. This makes the order decision simple: one day = Epic Universe, one day = IOA + USF.
Recommended order — subject to group discussion
Recommendation: May 20 Epic Universe · May 21 IOA + USF
May 20 (Wed) → Epic Universe: Fresh legs for the newest park. Midweek is one of the best crowd days. Loews Sapphire Falls (our hotel) is on the north campus — Epic Universe is on the south campus, so you'll need transportation between (park shuttle, rideshare, or drive). Early Park Admission (EPA) available for hotel guests: typically 8 AM entry for a 9 AM open.

May 21 (Thu) → Islands of Adventure + Universal Studios: Velocicoaster as the Savannah focus. IOA is walking distance from Sapphire Falls — easy EPA morning at 8 AM. Park hop to USF included on most tickets. City Walk is between both parks, making the Mandalorian & Grogu at 8 PM a natural cap to the day.

The Mandalorian anchor: If we attend the 8 PM showing on May 21 at City Walk AMC, IOA/USF is definitively May 21 (City Walk is on the north campus). Epic Universe is definitively May 20. This is the cleanest logical ordering.
